Make a pack

From an empty pack to pieces players can practise with the piano.

01 Your creator profile

Open the Creator and sign in with your Violin Hero account (or create a free one). Before your first pack you set up your creator profile: a creator name, a picture (your account's, or one you upload) and a few words about you: your education, where you teach or perform.

Players see your profile in the Marketplace with all your packs, so it is worth a minute. Write it in more than one language with the language tabs above the text; Edit profile changes it any time.

02 Start a pack

New content pack opens the editor. Give the pack a title and a description: what is in it, and who it is for. A picture (cropped to 16:9) shows with it here and in the Marketplace; drop one onto the preview or choose a file.

Texts in switches the language you are writing in: the pack's own language is marked main, and a text left empty in another language shows in the main one. Players see each pack in their language where you have written it.

Changes are kept as a draft until you press Save in the bar at the bottom; Discard drops them, and files you uploaded since go with them.

03 Add pieces

Add a piece for each piece in the pack, then fill in its card:

  • Title, composer and a short description: a sentence or two on what to listen for or practise. Title and composer are filled in from the score if empty.
  • Level, from Beginner to Expert, so players find pieces that suit them.
  • Genres: tick all that fit, for example an era and a form. The piece is found under each; the first ticked is its main genre. Add a new genre only if none fits.
  • The score (see Scores that work).

Listen beside the score plays the piece as Violin Hero will, violin and piano, so you can check it before anyone else does.

Players see the pieces in the order you give them: drag a card by its handle (or press ↑ ↓ on it). The triangle folds a card away to a single line, which keeps a long pack tidy.

04 Scores that work

Scores are MusicXML, exported from MuseScore, Dorico, Finale or Sibelius: uncompressed (.musicxml or .xml, not .mxl), at most 10 MB, with a violin part and usually a piano. Name the violin's part “Violin” (or give it the violin sound) so Violin Hero finds it.

Every score is checked as it is uploaded. Anything that stops it working (no violin part, a part without notes, a file that can't be read) is reported straight away. Smaller things are listed under the score, with their bars, so you can fix them in your notation program and upload it again:

  • no tempo (it then plays at 90 BPM) or no time signature at the start;
  • jumps (D.C., D.S., Fine, Coda) written only as text: use your program's jump marks, or playback won't follow them;
  • bars that don't add up, violin notes outside the violin's range, hidden notes;
  • no title or composer.

Some things are fixed for you and listed as Fixed: part names, stray tempo decimals, pedal marks, contradictory dynamics. Each uploaded score also gets a preview picture for the Marketplace: the violin part's first bars, faded and stamped so it can't be used as sheet music.

05 Interpretations

Interpretations are performances players can listen to and play along with: the piano then follows the performers' timing and dynamics. They are optional, but they make a piece come alive.

Add a recording

Your own recording as an audio file: MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG or FLAC, at most 40 MB. It is uploaded with the pack.

Add a YouTube video

Paste its link. Players watch it in Violin Hero while the score and the piano follow it.

Give each the performers and, if you like, notes: where and when it was recorded, what makes it special.

06 Interpretation data

For the score and the piano to follow a performance, Violin Hero needs to know where every bar falls in it: its tempo map, built once here and given to players with your pack. A recording you upload is lined up with the score straight away. For a video, choose one of two ways:

1Listen to the video

Start listening plays the video once in this tab while Violin Hero listens. Your browser asks what to share: choose this tab and turn on Also share tab audio. Let it play right to the end. Chrome or Edge on a computer; only the sound is analysed, on your computer.

2Upload its audio

Quicker, and more precise: the mix, or the violin and the piano on separate tracks. The audio must start exactly where the video starts, and be yours to share.

While it works, the card shows each stage. When it is done, a chip says how it went:

  • ✓ Tempo analysis done: lined up with the score.
  • ⚠ The violin's first note wasn't found: the timing may be off. Check it in Violin Hero, or build it again, best from uploaded audio.
  • ⚠ The introduction looks shortened: the players probably cut bars before the violin comes in, so the introduction can't be followed exactly. The violin's part should still be in time.
  • ⚠ The introduction looks longer than written: something was added before the violin comes in (a pause, repeated bars, talking), or its first note was found in the wrong place. Check it in Violin Hero.

Replace a piece's score and its interpretations are lined up again with the new one. Recordings are redone by themselves; a video needs listening to, or its audio, once more.

Publish and share

Put your pack in front of players, keep it up to date, and if you like, sell it.

07 Publish

A pack is a draft until you switch on Publish in the Violin Hero Marketplace. It needs a title and at least one piece with a score, and you confirm that you may share everything in it (see What you may upload). Then choose who can add it:

Everyone

The pack is in the Marketplace for all players, with your profile.

My students only

For teachers on Crescendu: only your students (and their parents) see it, under From your teacher. New students get it as they join.

Save, and it is live. The card on your page shows its version and how many players have added it.

08 Updates and versions

As long as no player has added a pack, you change it freely and can delete it. Once players have it, it is theirs too: saving then releases a new version. Write a line under What's new: players see it when Violin Hero offers them the update, and choose to update or keep their version.

A pack players have stays published and can't be deleted, so nobody loses pieces they practise. Recordings and scores you take out stay available to those on older versions.

09 Sell your packs

Packs can be free or sold. Crescendu sells them to players through Stripe, which takes the payment, charges the VAT and sends the invoice; you get 70 % of every sale's price before tax, paid out to your bank account.

  1. Under Sell your packs on your creator page, choose the country you'll be paid in and set up payouts with Stripe.
  2. Publish the pack to everyone and save it.
  3. Under the publish switch, set its price in euros, VAT included (0.99 € to 999.99 €). It shows in the Marketplace at once.

Your Stripe dashboard, linked from the same card, shows your sales and payouts.

10 What you may upload

Only add what you made yourself or may share. Old compositions are often free, but editions, arrangements, engravings and recordings usually are not. Never upload copies of printed music, CDs, streams or YouTube videos; a YouTube link is fine, as the video plays from YouTube.
