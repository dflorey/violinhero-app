Violin Hero How to use

How to use Violin Hero

Pieces for violin and piano, with the score scrolling past. Listen to them, then switch the violin off and play its part yourself, with the piano accompanying you.

▶ Open Violin Hero

01 Get started

  1. Pick a piece under Level, Genre and Piece. More pieces unlock as you practise. The Score key beside it opens the violin part as a PDF to print, with your marks (signed in).
  2. Press Play to hear it. The blue line on the score marks the note you hear; drag the score to move around.
  3. Put on wired headphones, so the microphone hears only your violin.
  4. Press Record and play the violin part, with the piano playing along (and a count-in if the violin starts right away).
  5. Press Record again (or play to the end). Your take plays back, and a full take gets a score.

02 The controls

  • Play starts and pauses. Back and Forward move one bar.
  • Record records you playing the violin part; Clear deletes the take that is chosen; Share sends it as a link.
  • Marker: press it at the first bar of a passage and again at its last, and the passage repeats until you switch Repeat off.
  • Input sets your microphone level. Aim for green while you play.
  • Tempo slows the piece down or speeds it up. Click adds a metronome.
  • Piano and Violin switch each part on or off; Staves hides the piano’s staves so the violin’s is larger.
  • Interpretation lets you hear real recordings of the piece and play along with them; your own takes are listed there too.

On a phone the controls are on three tabs at the bottom: Transport, Settings and Repertoire.

03 Your recordings

While you play, a line is drawn into the violin’s staff where your pitch is: green in tune, yellow to red the further off. Wrong and missed notes are circled, and little rewards pop up for good notes.

Record a piece from start to end and you get a score for intonation, timing, sound and more, with a tip on what to practise next.

Sign in (the round key at the top right) to keep your takes and progress, share takes and appear in the top scores. Everything else works without an account.

Use wired headphones while recording. With speakers the microphone hears the piano too, and Bluetooth headphones lag behind.

04 Notes on the score

Click notes in the violin part to add fingerings, bowings, staccato, accents or tenuto; on one note also a dynamic or a fermata, between two (Shift-click the second, or click a slur) a slur, < > or rit. The violin plays them, and ↺ Edited above the score takes them all off again.
